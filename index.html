Learn Smarter

Connect with local tutors for personalized learning experiences.

Tutor Search

Allows students to search for and filter local tutors based on subject, availability, and price.

Scheduling Tool

Enables students to book and manage tutoring sessions with their chosen tutors.

In-App Messaging

Provides a secure and convenient way for students and tutors to communicate and share resources.

Payment Processing

Facilitates secure and easy payment for tutoring sessions, with options for one-time or recurring payments.

Rating and Review System

Allows students to rate and review their tutors, helping to build a reputation system and ensure high-quality tutoring experiences.
